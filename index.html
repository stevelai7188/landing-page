FAQ

Questions owners ask us.

How much does it cost?
Projects typically start from RM 3,888 for a growth website and RM 6,888 for a full growth system. You get a fixed quote in your free audit — before we start, not after.
How long will it take?
Most landing-page projects launch within 7 days. Full growth systems (website + ads + automation) take 2–4 weeks depending on scope. You get a live preview link from day one.
Is this for small businesses?
Yes — most of our clients are small and medium businesses. We scale the system to your budget: start with one landing page and one campaign, then expand once it's producing leads.
Do you handle hosting and domains?
Yes. Hosting, domain setup, SSL and deployment are all included. You own everything — domain, site, ad accounts and automation workflows.
Do you manage the ads?
We can. Campaign setup, daily creative testing and optimization are part of the Growth System. Ad spend goes directly to Meta/Google — no markup on your media budget.
How does the WhatsApp automation work?
When a lead submits a form or messages you, the AI qualifies them instantly — asks the right questions, answers FAQs, and books a call. You only get warm, qualified conversations. Your number stays yours.
What happens after launch?
Weekly optimization: what's working, what's next, and the numbers behind it. You can stay hands-off or jump in — everything is in a dashboard you can see.
