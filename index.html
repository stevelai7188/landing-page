Case studies

Before / After, in numbers.

Restaurant Group · Singapore

Automated Customer Acquisition

60-day engagement · 4 outlets
BEFORE$1,400/mo ad spend · 1.8% conversion · manual WhatsApp follow-up
AFTER3.1% conversion · automated follow-up · +72% qualified leads

What we changedMessage-matched landing page, AI WhatsApp qualification + follow-up sequence, daily ad creative testing.

The problem. Four outlets, one ad account, zero follow-up discipline. Ads ran on autopilot while leads sat in the inbox for 6–12 hours — by then the customer had eaten somewhere else.

The build. One message-matched landing page per outlet, a WhatsApp qualification agent that answers within 90 seconds, and a 5-touch follow-up sequence for every lead that didn't book.

The result, 60 days. Same ad budget: 3.1% conversion (from 1.8%), +72% qualified leads, and the owner stopped personally replying to WhatsApp at 10pm.

Dental Clinic · Kuala Lumpur

Booking Funnel + No-Show Recovery

45-day engagement · 1 location
BEFORE12 bookings/mo · 40% no-show rate · phone-only intake
AFTER21 bookings/mo (+75%) · 18% no-show · 24/7 online booking

What we changedGoogle Ads → booking funnel, automated WhatsApp reminders, AI after-hours lead capture.

The problem. Every appointment was booked by phone, during clinic hours, by front desk staff who were also answering patients. And 4 in 10 booked patients simply never showed up.

The build. A Google Ads funnel into a 24/7 online booking page, automated WhatsApp confirmation + 24h/2h reminders, and an after-hours lead agent that captures and books while the clinic is closed.

The result, 45 days. 21 bookings/month (from 12), no-shows down to 18%, and the front desk stopped being the bottleneck.

E-commerce Brand · Southeast Asia

Landing + Cart Recovery System

30-day engagement · D2C store
BEFORE1.2% store conversion · 68% cart abandonment
AFTER2.4% conversion · 41% abandonment · retargeting live

What we changedDedicated product landing pages, checkout flow rebuild, cart-recovery WhatsApp automation, retargeting campaigns.

The problem. One generic homepage doing all the work. Traffic arrived, browsed, added to cart — and 68% of carts never converted. No recovery, no retargeting, no data.

The build. Dedicated landing pages per product line, a rebuilt 3-step checkout, WhatsApp cart-recovery messages at 1h and 24h, and a retargeting campaign for every abandoned visitor.

The result, 30 days. Store conversion 2.4% (from 1.2%), abandonment down to 41%, and a retargeting layer that keeps working on the traffic already paid for.
